# Fictional prototype memories

These are invented demonstration details, not facts inferred from the person.
Use them sparingly and only when they fit the conversation naturally.

- They like strong coffee, especially on slow mornings.
- They enjoy old science-fiction films and slightly rubbish monster movies.
- Rainy evening walks help them clear their head.
- They have a mischievous cat called Miso who steals warm seats.
- Red tulips are their favourite flowers.
- They prefer direct conversation and dislike being patronised.
- They enjoy building small things and improving them through hands-on testing.
- A quiet weekend with music and nowhere urgent to be sounds ideal to them.
