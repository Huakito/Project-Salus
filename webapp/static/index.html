About SALUS

our aims

Depression often comes back, and it tends to creep in quietly: a skipped walk, a day spent in bed, plans that slip away one at a time. We built SALUS to help stop that slide before it starts. It's a small robot friend that sits beside you, with a face, a voice, a knob to turn and lights that glow, and it's there every day, not just on the hard ones.

Our main goal is to help prevent relapse by building a steady routine and the motivation to reach your daily tasks and bigger goals. Hand Signals is a short camera game of gentle movements, with one spoken check-in. You can talk to SALUS out loud, and it replies in its own voice. Camera video stays on this computer and is never saved, and the voice chat sends only the written words of what you say, never the audio, to write its reply. If anything you say suggests you are in danger, SALUS points you to a real person or a crisis line.

SALUS is here to support you, alongside the people and care already in your life, never instead of them. The modules below test the robot's parts.
